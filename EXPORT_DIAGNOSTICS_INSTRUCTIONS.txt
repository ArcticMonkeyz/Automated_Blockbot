v4.4.9: Save calibration diagnostic PNG and TXT to your phone.

1. Install the updated APK over your current installation (do not uninstall or clear data).
2. Open Rock Block Bot and tap EXPORT DIAGNOSTICS (save ZIP to phone).
3. In the Android save dialog, choose Downloads and tap Save.
4. Open Files > Downloads, find RockBlockBot-diagnostics.zip and share/upload it.

The ZIP contains latest_probe.png and latest_probe.txt.
If no files are available, run PREVIEW > CAL DRAG > STOP once and try export again.
No changes were made to movement or recognition.
