RockBlockBot v4.4.11 diagnostic files

After PREVIEW, tap CAL DRAG once. Wait until the full gesture has finished.
To export, go back to RockBlockBot, then tap EXPORT DIAGNOSTICS and save to Downloads.
The output ZIP includes latest_probe.png and latest_probe.txt.
For horizontal purple 4-strips it should ALSO include latest_probe_b.png
and latest_probe_b.txt, captured after the one-cell second probe movement.
If one capture is missing, the time window may have been skipped, or Android
may have interrupted the gesture. DO NOT force a NEXT MOVE to collect diagnostics.
Do not uninstall or clear app data before exporting.
